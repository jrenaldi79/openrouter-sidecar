Ask. Explore. Bring back.

A second opinion without filling your main chat.

  1. Ask

    Your main chat sends the task and the context you choose.

  2. Explore

    The advisor works in a separate chat with its own history.

  3. Bring back

    A selection, the full reply or a summary. Or Save it as a file.

Your skills come along. Your main chat can pass shareable skill instructions to the advisor.

What gets shared?

Only the context and skill instructions you choose. Your main chat's history, plugins and private files are not automatically sent.
